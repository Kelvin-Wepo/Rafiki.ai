SECTION 1: eCITIZEN PORTAL OVERVIEW
====================================

What is eCitizen?
------------------
eCitizen is Kenya's online government services portal that enables citizens to access various government services from the comfort of their homes or offices. It provides a single point of access to multiple government services.

Key Features
-------------
- 24/7 online access to government services
- Secure payment processing via M-Pesa and cards
- Application tracking
- Digital document delivery
- SMS and email notifications

Registration
-------------
Step 1: Visit https://www.ecitizen.go.ke
Step 2: Click "Create Account"
Step 3: Fill in personal details:
   - Full names
   - National ID number
   - Email address
   - Phone number
Step 4: Verify email and phone
Step 5: Set password
Step 6: Complete profile


SECTION 2: NATIONAL ID SERVICES
================================

First Time Application (Age 18+)
---------------------------------
Requirements:
1. Birth certificate (original)
2. Parents' ID copies
3. Completed application form

Process:
1. Visit nearest Huduma Centre or Chief's office
2. Fill application form
3. Provide biometrics (fingerprints, photo)
4. Receive waiting card
5. Collect ID when ready (4-6 weeks)

Lost/Damaged ID Replacement
----------------------------
Requirements:
1. Police abstract (if lost)
2. Old ID or copy (if damaged)
3. Completed application form

Process (Online via eCitizen):
1. Log in to eCitizen
2. Select "National Registration Bureau"
3. Choose "ID Replacement"
4. Fill application form
5. Upload police abstract (if lost)
6. Pay fee: KES 100
7. Track application
8. Collect at designated centre

ID Collection Tracking
-----------------------
Online: https://www.ecitizen.go.ke/id-status
USSD: *278# (follow prompts)
Call: 020 222 0627


SECTION 3: PASSPORT SERVICES
=============================

Types of Passports
-------------------
1. Ordinary Passport (Blue)
   - 32 pages: KES 4,550
   - 48 pages: KES 6,050
   - Validity: 10 years (adults), 5 years (children)

2. Diplomatic Passport (Red)
   - Issued to government officials

3. East African Passport
   - For travel within EAC member states

First Time Application
-----------------------
Requirements:
1. National ID card (original + copy)
2. Birth certificate (original + copy)
3. 2 passport-size photos (white background)
4. Completed application form
5. Payment receipt

Online Process:
1. Log in to eCitizen
2. Select "Immigration Services"
3. Choose "Passport Application"
4. Fill application form
5. Upload documents
6. Pay processing fee
7. Book appointment
8. Visit immigration office with originals
9. Provide biometrics
10. Track application
11. Collect passport (10-14 working days)

Passport Renewal
-----------------
Requirements:
1. Current passport (must be valid or recently expired)
2. National ID card
3. 2 passport photos
4. Payment receipt

Processing Time: 10-14 working days
Express Service: 3-5 working days (additional fee)

Tracking Passport Application
------------------------------
Online: https://www.ecitizen.go.ke/passport-status
Call: +254 20 222 2022


SECTION 4: DRIVING LICENSE SERVICES
====================================

Types of Licenses
------------------
- Class A: Motorcycle
- Class B: Light vehicles (up to 3,500 kg)
- Class C: Heavy vehicles (above 3,500 kg)
- Class D: Articulated vehicles
- Class E: Special vehicles
- Class F: Tractors
- Class G: PSV (Public Service Vehicles)

First Time Application
-----------------------
Requirements:
1. Valid National ID
2. Provisional driving license (PDL)
3. Certificate from approved driving school
4. Passed driving test certificate
5. Medical fitness certificate
6. 2 passport photos

Process:
1. Obtain Provisional License (PDL):
   - Apply via eCitizen
   - Fee: KES 600
   - Valid for 1 year

2. Driving School Training:
   - Minimum 30 days
   - Theory and practical lessons

3. Driving Test:
   - Book via eCitizen
   - Practical driving test at NTSA centre
   - Test fee: KES 200

4. Apply for Full License:
   - Via eCitizen after passing test
   - License fee: KES 1,050
   - Smart card: KES 850

License Renewal
----------------
- Renewal required before expiry
- Online via eCitizen
- Fee: KES 1,050 + smart card KES 850
- Validity: 3 years

Lost License Replacement
-------------------------
1. Obtain police abstract
2. Apply via eCitizen
3. Upload police abstract
4. Pay replacement fee
5. Collect new license


SECTION 5: CERTIFICATE OF GOOD CONDUCT
=======================================

What is it?
------------
The Certificate of Good Conduct (Police Clearance Certificate) is an official document issued by the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) certifying that a person has no criminal record in Kenya.

Required For:
- Employment (public and private)
- Immigration purposes
- Professional licensing
- Education applications abroad
- Visa applications

Application Process
--------------------
Step 1: Online Application
   - Visit eCitizen or DCI website
   - Fill application form
   - Upload passport photo
   - Pay fee: KES 1,050

Step 2: Biometrics Capture
   - Book appointment via system
   - Visit DCI office or Huduma Centre
   - Provide fingerprints

Step 3: Processing
   - Wait 2-4 weeks for processing
   - Background verification

Step 4: Collection
   - Collect at designated centre
   - Can also check status online

Validity Period
----------------
- International use: 6 months
- Local use: varies by institution (typically 1 year)


SECTION 6: BUSINESS REGISTRATION SERVICES
==========================================

Business Name Registration
---------------------------
Process:
1. Search for name availability on eCitizen
2. Reserve business name (KES 150)
3. Submit registration documents
4. Pay registration fee (KES 1,000)
5. Receive certificate

Requirements:
- Proposed business name
- National ID copies of owners
- Physical address
- Nature of business

Company Registration (Limited Company)
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
1. Minimum 1 director
2. Minimum 1 shareholder
3. Registered office address
4. Constitution (Articles of Association)
5. Consent forms from directors
6. KRA PIN for directors

Fees:
- Name reservation: KES 150
- Registration: KES 10,000 (nominal capital up to KES 100,000)
- Additional fees for higher capital

Processing Time: 3-7 working days


SECTION 7: LAND SERVICES
=========================

Title Search
-------------
Purpose: Verify ownership and encumbrances on land
Process:
1. Visit eCitizen or lands registry
2. Provide title deed number
3. Pay search fee: KES 520
4. Receive search results

Land Rent Payment
------------------
1. Log in to eCitizen
2. Select "Ministry of Lands"
3. Choose "Land Rent Payment"
4. Enter parcel number
5. View amount due
6. Pay via M-Pesa or card

Land Rates Clearance
---------------------
Obtained from county government
Required for land transactions

SECTION 10: HUDUMA CENTRES
===========================

What is Huduma Kenya?
----------------------
Huduma Kenya is a government initiative providing integrated public services under one roof. Citizens can access multiple government services at any Huduma Centre nationwide.

Services Available
-------------------
- National ID application/replacement
- KRA PIN registration
- NHIF registration
- NSSF registration
- Business registration
- Driving license services
- Birth/death certificate services
- Police abstract
- And many more

Operating Hours
----------------
Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM
Saturday: 8:00 AM - 1:00 PM
No lunch break (continuous service)

Major Huduma Centres
---------------------
Nairobi:
- GPO Huduma Centre, Haile Selassie Avenue
- City Square, City Hall Way
- Eastleigh, Eastleigh 1st Avenue

Mombasa:
- Huduma Centre Mombasa, Uhuru na Kazi Building

Kisumu:
- Huduma Centre Kisumu, Mega Plaza

Nakuru:
- Huduma Centre Nakuru, County Commissioner's Office

And in all 47 counties
