SECTION 1: KRA PIN REGISTRATION
================================

What is a KRA PIN?
-------------------
A KRA PIN (Personal Identification Number) is a unique identifier issued by the Kenya Revenue Authority to every taxpayer in Kenya. It is required for:
- Filing tax returns
- Opening bank accounts
- Registering businesses
- Applying for government jobs
- Land transactions
- Vehicle registration
- Importing and exporting goods

Eligibility for KRA PIN
------------------------
Any person or entity that earns income in Kenya must register for a KRA PIN, including:
- Kenyan citizens aged 18 years and above
- Foreign nationals working or doing business in Kenya
- Companies and partnerships registered in Kenya
- Non-profit organizations

Documents Required for KRA PIN Registration (Individual)
---------------------------------------------------------
1. Valid National ID card or Passport
2. Two recent passport-size photographs
3. Completed PIN application form (available on iTax)
4. Supporting documents based on income source:
   - Employment: Letter from employer
   - Business: Business registration certificate
   - Rental income: Property ownership documents

How to Apply for KRA PIN Online (iTax)
---------------------------------------
Step 1: Visit https://itax.kra.go.ke
Step 2: Click on "New PIN Registration"
Step 3: Select taxpayer type (Individual/Non-Individual)
Step 4: Fill in personal details:
   - Full names (as per National ID)
   - Date of birth
   - National ID number
   - Email address (active)
   - Mobile phone number
Step 5: Upload required documents
Step 6: Submit application
Step 7: Receive PIN via email within 24-48 hours

PIN Registration at Huduma Centres
-----------------------------------
Location: Any Huduma Centre nationwide
Requirements: Original National ID + 2 passport photos
Processing time: Same day (if no complications)
Fee: FREE


SECTION 2: KRA PIN RECOVERY
============================

Forgot Your KRA PIN?
---------------------
If you have forgotten your KRA PIN, you can recover it through:

Method 1: Online Recovery via iTax
-----------------------------------
Step 1: Visit https://itax.kra.go.ke
Step 2: Click "Forgot PIN"
Step 3: Enter your National ID number
Step 4: Enter registered email address
Step 5: Click "Retrieve"
Step 6: Check email for PIN (sent to registered email)

Method 2: USSD Recovery
------------------------
Dial *285# on your registered mobile number
Follow the prompts to receive your PIN via SMS

Method 3: KRA Contact Centre
-----------------------------
Call: +254 (020) 4 999 999
Toll-free: 0800 222 222
Email: callcentre@kra.go.ke
Provide: Full name, National ID, date of birth

Method 4: Visit KRA Office
---------------------------
Visit any KRA service centre with:
- Original National ID
- Completed request form


SECTION 3: NIL RETURNS FILING
==============================

What are Nil Returns?
----------------------
Nil Returns are tax returns filed when a person has a KRA PIN but has no taxable income to declare for a given tax period. Filing Nil Returns is MANDATORY even if you earned no income.

Who Must File Nil Returns?
---------------------------
- Employees whose PAYE is deducted at source (already filed by employer)
- Unemployed individuals with KRA PIN
- Students with KRA PIN
- Retirees with no taxable income
- Persons between jobs

Filing Deadline
----------------
Annual returns (including Nil Returns) must be filed by 30th June each year for the previous tax year (January 1 - December 31).

Penalty for Late/Non-Filing
----------------------------
- Penalty: KES 2,000 per month or 5% of tax due (whichever is higher)
- Maximum: KES 20,000 per return
- Interest: 1% per month on unpaid tax

How to File Nil Returns on iTax
--------------------------------
Step 1: Log in to https://itax.kra.go.ke
   - Enter your KRA PIN
   - Enter your password
   - Complete security verification

Step 2: Navigate to Returns
   - Click "Returns" on the main menu
   - Select "File Returns"

Step 3: Select Return Type
   - Choose "Income Tax - Resident Individual"
   - Select the tax year (e.g., 2023 for filing in 2024)

Step 4: Fill in the Form
   - For Nil Returns, enter ZERO (0) in all income fields
   - Employment income: 0
   - Business income: 0
   - Rental income: 0
   - Other income: 0

Step 5: Submit Return
   - Review all entries
   - Click "Submit"
   - Save or print the acknowledgement receipt

Step 6: Confirm Submission
   - Check "My Returns" to confirm status shows "Filed"
   - Download the acknowledgement for your records


SECTION 4: iTAX PASSWORD MANAGEMENT
====================================

First Time Login
-----------------
1. Visit https://itax.kra.go.ke
2. Click "First Time Login"
3. Enter your KRA PIN
4. Enter your registered email
5. Click "Generate Password"
6. Check email for temporary password
7. Log in with temporary password
8. Create new password (must include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, special characters)

Password Reset
---------------
1. Click "Forgot Password" on iTax login page
2. Enter KRA PIN
3. Enter registered email
4. Click "Reset Password"
5. Check email for reset link
6. Create new password

Password Requirements
----------------------
- Minimum 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
- At least one lowercase letter (a-z)
- At least one number (0-9)
- At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)


SECTION 5: KRA TAX COMPLIANCE CERTIFICATE (TCC)
================================================

What is a Tax Compliance Certificate?
--------------------------------------
A Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC) is a document issued by KRA confirming that a taxpayer has met all tax obligations. It is required for:
- Government tenders and contracts
- Renewal of business licenses
- Professional registration
- Certain visa applications
- Banking and financial services

Validity Period
----------------
- Valid for 12 months from date of issue
- Must be renewed annually

Requirements for TCC
---------------------
1. All tax returns filed up to date
2. No outstanding tax liability
3. No pending tax disputes
4. Active KRA PIN status

How to Apply for TCC Online
----------------------------
Step 1: Log in to iTax
Step 2: Click "Certificates" > "Apply for Certificate"
Step 3: Select "Tax Compliance Certificate"
Step 4: Confirm tax status (system checks automatically)
Step 5: Submit application
Step 6: Download TCC (if eligible) or view status

Processing Time
----------------
- Instant: If all returns filed and no outstanding liability
- Up to 7 days: If review required
- May require visit: If tax disputes exist
