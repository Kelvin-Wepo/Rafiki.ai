Criterion D1–D3 · Ethics, Safety & Responsibility · A2 Self-Reliance

Six-Pillar National Security Framework

Rafiki.ai is not just a chatbot — it is a security enforcement layer protecting citizen data and national digital infrastructure.

1

Confidentiality — AES-256 + TLS 1.3

Zero-knowledge architecture. Citizen PII is encrypted at rest and in transit. 30-minute session auto-expiry.

2

Integrity — Fraud Detection

AI-powered anomaly detection, bot analysis, input validation. SHA-256 hash-chained immutable audit trail.

3

Authenticity — Constitutional RAG

All legal guidance cross-referenced with official statutes. Digital signatures on documents. Source citations always provided.

4

Availability — High-Availability Design

DDoS protection, automated failover, multi-backend architecture. Works on 2G / edge / low-connectivity environments.

5

Accountability — RBAC + Audit Logs

Role-based access control, user behavior analytics, insider-threat detection. Anonymous whistleblower channel.

6

Legitimacy — Data Protection Act 2019

Privacy-by-design. Constitutional rights verification. Full compliance with Kenya's national data governance framework.

🔍

Transparency & Explainability

Every AI decision logged with source citations. Operators can audit and reconstruct the reasoning chain for any interaction.

⚖️

Bias & Fairness

Constitutional grounding prevents hallucination. Multilingual support (Kiswahili + English) ensures no tribe or region is left behind.

🇰🇪

100% Sovereign Data

All citizen data processed and stored within Kenyan-controlled infrastructure. No extraterritorial data exposure.
